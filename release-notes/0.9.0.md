Restling 0.9.0 — reminders that fit your day.

- A gentle heads-up before a break, with 3- or 5-minute snooze from the heads-up, break screen or menu bar. The heads-up does not take keyboard focus; snoozing does not earn forest growth.
- Optional working days and local reminder hours, including overnight windows. Outside your hours, overlays and sounds stay quiet; the next eligible window begins a fresh use interval.
- Temporary do-not-disturb for 30 minutes or 1 hour, with a visible recovery time and an option to resume early.
- Optional launch at login, off until you enable it. Saved timer state restores running/paused intention and remaining time after relaunch, without counting offline time or replaying missed rewards.
- Idle and lock handling with a return grace period. Idle detection defaults to 5 minutes and return grace to 30 seconds; both are configurable. Lock or idle starts a fresh use interval, while system/display sleep freezes the current timer. Snoozes that expire during sleep or a long app stall are discarded. Keeping still during a break does not interrupt it.
- Independent start and end sound controls; previews are silent, and reminder restrictions also suppress sounds.
- A unified settings window with draft Save/Cancel for reminder preferences. Login-item changes apply immediately through macOS and may require system approval.

The default rhythm remains 20 minutes / 20 seconds. Existing local forests, profiles and unlocks are preserved. No camera, gaze verification, account or cloud sync is added.

Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. Interface language: Simplified Chinese.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Developer ID signing and notarization remain deferred.
