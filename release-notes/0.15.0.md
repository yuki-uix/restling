Restling 0.15.0 — little forest encounters.

- A little red bird, a little blue bird or a cluster of golden mushrooms can now appear after a completed rest. They remain in that week's forest, alongside the existing weekly ecology milestones.
- Each local forest gets one saved 25% chance per day with a completed rest, with at most two different encounters in a week. If the first two eligible days have no encounter, the third guarantees one. Days do not need to be consecutive; additional rests on the same day do not add chances.
- Open a week in “森林年鉴” to see its encounter dates and short notes. Both successful and unsuccessful daily decisions are saved, so reopening the app, browsing the yearbook or restoring a backup does not reroll them. Historical records are not backfilled with encounters.
- Birds occasionally tilt their heads in the normal forest window. Reduce Motion freezes the animation. The 20-second rest overlay stays free of encounter scenery, prompts and extra tasks; encounters add no growth, trees, currency or achievement points.
- Forest archive version 4 stores encounters and daily decisions inside backup envelope version 1. Valid older local archives and portable backups remain readable. Before upgrading a local version 3 archive, its original bytes are preserved in local preferences. Version 4 archives and backups require Restling 0.15.0 or later; Restling 0.14.0 cannot read them.

Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. Interface language: Simplified Chinese.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Developer ID signing and notarization remain deferred.
