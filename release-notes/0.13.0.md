Restling 0.13.0 — forest footprints, at your own pace.

- Working-day and time restrictions now have an explicit “启用工作日与时段限制” switch. The restriction remains off by default. When it is off, day and time controls are hidden and reminders can run on any day and at any hour, subject to your other reminder choices.
- Turning the restriction off retains the selected days and hours for later. These edits remain a draft until saved; cancelling leaves saved preferences unchanged. Upgrading preserves an existing enabled or disabled restriction. Do-not-disturb, away detection, manual pause and other reminder rules continue to apply.
- Open “森林足迹” from the main forest card or menu bar to explore 18 achievements across first growth, exploration, planted tree variety, ecological discoveries and time for yourself. Cards show stories, conditions and progress for the current forest.
- Footprints recognize the first rest and tree, existing flowers and stream, mushrooms, a full grove at 48 rests, new groves at 49 and 97, 100 completed rests, 2/4/6 actually planted mature tree species, the existing pond/bridge/visitors at 36/72/120 rests, and 10 minutes/1 hour/3 hours of completed rest timers.
- Achievements are derived from existing forest data. They add no scenery, growth rewards, unlock dates or event records. They need no claiming or consecutive-day attendance, do not interrupt a rest, and do not reset because you take time away. Selecting or unlocking a tree species alone does not earn a planted-variety achievement.
- Existing forests, settings, completed rests, trees and records are preserved without a new archive schema. Each forest's footprints are independent. Restoring an older backup shows the progress present in that restored snapshot. Time achievements describe completed timers, not verified gaze or health outcomes.
- The default 20-minute / 20-second rhythm, whole-screen 12% breathing light, Esc and snooze behavior, and three-rest tree-growth rule remain unchanged. Previews and unfinished rests still earn no growth. No account, network service or new system permission is introduced by these features.

Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. Interface language: Simplified Chinese.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Developer ID signing and notarization remain deferred.
