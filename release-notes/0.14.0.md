Restling 0.14.0 — this week's forest, a yearbook to return to.

- Cultivation now has a slower rhythm. A new forest's first tree still grows over three completed rests; each subsequent tree takes 24. An unfinished old three-rest cycle keeps its original target until it finishes, then uses the new rhythm. Changing species preserves cultivation progress.
- Each Monday–Sunday week has its own forest, using the local Gregorian calendar. Opening a new week displays empty land without awarding growth or saving an attendance record. Only a real completed rest adds that week to history. Weeks with one or two rests and no mature tree are retained; unused weeks are not invented and recorded weeks need not be consecutive.
- Unfinished plants continue across weeks. A mature tree stays in the week in which it finishes growing. Historical weeks keep their stored cultivation snapshot, original date range and timezone. Clock or timezone rollback keeps cultivation in the latest recorded week rather than reopening an archived one.
- Open “森林年鉴” from the main forest card or menu bar to browse week thumbnails grouped by year and month, inspect full forests, and review completed-rest counts, completed-timer duration, mature trees and species. These are timer records, not gaze or health measurements.
- “布置本周林地” lets you move or swap already mature trees in the current week. Trees are planted automatically; arranging them is optional and changes save immediately. Historical forests are read-only. More than 16 mature trees in a high-volume week use additional pages within the same week without losing trees.
- All existing trees, IDs, species, seed, old grove order/layout, cumulative progress, detailed events and earned footprints are preserved as “最初的森林”. Old dates are not used to fabricate weekly forests. Existing tree-species unlock thresholds remain unchanged.
- “森林足迹” now has 22 keepsakes. Four new footprints celebrate 4, 12 and 26 different recorded weeks, and three different mature species within one weekly forest. Existing achievement identities and earned footprints are retained; land-related progress now follows actual mature trees and recorded weeks. No consecutive attendance, claiming or extra reward path is added.
- Future ecological discoveries follow 3, 6 and 10 different weeks with completed rests and stay in the week that earns them. A pond, bridge or visitors already earned under the old 36/72/120-rest rules remain in their original groves. The rest overlay remains free of new ecological tasks.
- Forest archive version 3 stores weekly history, layouts and cross-week cultivation inside backup envelope version 1. Valid old local archives and portable backups remain readable. Restoring an older backup preserves its forest and begins weekly cultivation without backfilling weeks. Weekly archives and their backups require Restling 0.14.0 or later; earlier versions cannot read them.
- The default 20-minute / 20-second reminder rhythm, optional working-day/time restriction, 12% whole-screen breathing light, Esc, snooze, local backup/recovery and reminder preferences remain. Previews and unfinished rests earn no growth. No account, new system permission, analytics or network service is introduced.

Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. Interface language: Simplified Chinese.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Developer ID signing and notarization remain deferred.
