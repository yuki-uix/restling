Restling 0.11.0 — a forest with places to return to.

- Three lasting ecological discoveries follow the early forest growth: a pond in grove 1 at 36 completed rests, a stone bridge in grove 2 at 72, and rabbit visitors in grove 3 at 120. Conditions use this forest's cumulative completed rests.
- Open “林地手记” from the forest card or menu bar to see conditions, progress and the next discovery. Unlocked entries can take you back to their grove; landmarks stay there when a new grove begins.
- Birch unlocks at 12 rests, willow at 36 and ginkgo at 72. Each has a distinct silhouette and its own sprout, sapling and mature artwork. All six species, including locked ones, can be browsed in the expanded catalogue.
- Choosing another unlocked species retains the current growth stage. Existing mature trees keep their species, shapes and positions; the first three rests, early flowers/stream/mushrooms, three-rest tree rhythm and 48-rest grove expansion remain unchanged.
- New scenery and visitors appear in the normal forest window after a break, not in the rest overlay. There are no new rest-screen actions, claims, streaks or time-limited visits. Reduce Motion freezes their animation.
- Existing cumulative progress automatically qualifies for the appropriate scenery and species. Upgrading, previewing and visiting do not manufacture rest events or growth.
- Local records, export/restore and safety-copy recovery remain available. Old valid backups stay readable; backups containing birch, willow or ginkgo require Restling 0.11.0 or later. No new account, data collection, permission or background task is added.

This release completes Epic #19 (#9 and #10), following the local data and backup work delivered in Epic #18. Cross-device features and Apple notarization remain separate planned work.

The default rhythm remains 20 minutes / 20 seconds. Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later. Interface language: Simplified Chinese.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Developer ID signing and notarization remain deferred.
